About

Why I build.

I'm a sophomore at Harvard College studying electrical engineering. I got here through machines — figuring out how they move, why they fail, and what it takes to make them reliable enough to actually trust.

What draws me in is the process itself: taking a concept from a rough sketch through CAD, into a real prototype, and testing it until it actually holds up the way the drawing said it would. I'm early in that path right now, building the mechanical and electrical fundamentals through coursework and hands-on projects, machine-shop time included.

GOAL — a career in product development: designing parts and systems built to work reliably in the real world.
